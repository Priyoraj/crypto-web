Encryption & Decryption Web Application

Encrypt and decrypt text with three classic ciphers. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

  • Caesar cipher: every letter moves the same number of places along the alphabet.
  • Playfair cipher: pairs of letters are encrypted with a 5×5 key square, which is shown on the page.
  • DES and Triple DES: the 64-bit block cipher, with the key schedule and all 16 rounds listed step by step.
