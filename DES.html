DES / Triple DES Block Cipher

Encrypts or decrypts one 64-bit block: a message of 16 hexadecimal digits or exactly 8 characters, with keys of 16 hexadecimal digits. Triple DES here uses two keys: encrypt with key A, decrypt with key B, then encrypt with key A again. The details box lists the key schedule and every round.
